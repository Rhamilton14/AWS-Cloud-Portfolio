# Siemens: Rail Electrification Milestone and Budget Review

### Objective
Act as the project manager on a rail electrification program: review status updates from the team and report on progress, budget and stakeholder concerns.

### What I did
I reviewed updates from engineering, community relations and field operations, then pulled out the key progress figures (30 of 68 miles electrified, with track sections at 50% and 67%) and the budget risk ($90M spent, forecast $125M). I also identified issues to escalate, such as Eastern Section terrain delays and nighttime noise complaints.

### Skills I practiced
- **Project status reporting**: summarizing progress against plan
- **Budget tracking**: spotting forecast overruns and their causes
- **Stakeholder communication**: balancing engineering, community and field concerns

## Scenario
As the project manager ("Forager"), I received three status updates:

| From | Topic | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering Team Lead | Electrification and budget | 30 of 68 Phase 1 miles electrified (+6 this week). $90M spent, forecast $125M, slightly over budget because of Eastern Section terrain |
| Community Relations | Community feedback | More nighttime noise complaints in the Eastern Section; positive response to community engagement |
| Field Supervisor | Track installation | Eastern Section stalled at 6 of 12 miles (rocky terrain). Southeastern Quadrant at 10 of 15 miles (+4) after an equipment upgrade |
